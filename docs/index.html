Bible App

An offline Bible presenter for churches. No internet required after installation.

  • Project verses to a second screen or projector instantly
  • AI voice transcription — speak a reference, the app finds it
  • Control everything from your phone over WiFi or hotspot
  • Includes multiple Bible translations, all stored locally
  • Recordings saved to %APPDATA%\com.kyere.bible-app\
